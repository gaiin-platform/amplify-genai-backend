# Campus AI rollout proposal

## User
Create a presentation for IT leadership proposing a campus-wide rollout of our AI assistant platform.

## Assistant
Adoption since the January launch: 1,200 monthly active users in January, 1,650 in February, 2,100 in March, 2,600 in April, 3,100 in May and 3,700 in June. A spring faculty survey (n=412) found a median of 4.2 hours saved per week. Central hosting keeps data inside university AWS accounts, pools model spend, and gives admins control over which models are available; departmental licenses duplicate seats and send data off campus. Proposed phases: pilot with two colleges in Q1 (4,000 users, $120K), expand to all faculty in Q2-Q3 (12,000 users, $310K), then campus-wide including students and staff in Q4 (30,000 users, $640K). Risks: model cost growth (mitigated by rate limits), change management (training program), and data governance (existing data classification policy applies). Ask: approve $640K for FY27.
